As the Principal and Founder of Eman Educational Complex, Mustain Hasnain Shah oversees the academic and administrative functioning of the school. His responsibilities include:

  • 01 Supervising the school's academic activities
  • 02 Supervising the teaching staff
  • 03 Maintaining and overseeing student discipline
  • 04 Coordinating with parents regarding school matters
  • 05 Preparing specific study planning for classes whose students are appearing in board examinations
  • 06 Overseeing the overall academic and administrative functioning of the school
